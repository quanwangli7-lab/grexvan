Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy describes the website features currently implemented. Any future online inquiry form or analytics service will require a policy review before activation.

Who we are

GREXVAN provides product information, Amazon retail links, business inquiry contacts and installation support for its automotive maintenance products. For privacy questions, email support@grexvan.com.

Website requests and technical information

The website is hosted through Cloudflare Pages. When you visit, technical information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information and request time may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver and protect the website. Provider processing and retention are governed by the provider’s applicable terms and settings.

Email inquiries

Our contact links open your own email application. Clicking a link does not submit a website form or send an email automatically. If you choose to email us, we receive the information you include, such as your name, email address, company, vehicle details, order information and attachments. We use it to address your inquiry and maintain relevant support or business correspondence.

Customer service: support@grexvan.com. Business inquiries: sales@grexvan.com. Our business email is provided through Lark. Please do not send payment card details, passwords or unnecessary personal information.

Amazon and other external links

Retail purchases are completed on Amazon. GREXVAN does not collect payment card information or process checkout on this website. Amazon and other linked services apply their own privacy policies. Clicking an Amazon link is not proof of a completed purchase.

Installation videos

Installation videos are served as MP4 files through our Cloudflare Pages hosting and played using your browser’s native controls. Your browser may request video metadata before playback; videos do not autoplay. Video requests may involve the technical information described above. We do not store playback progress or send playback events to a third-party analytics service. No YouTube player or thumbnail is embedded on these video pages. If you choose an optional YouTube backup link, it opens YouTube in a new tab and YouTube applies its own privacy policies and technologies. Written installation guides remain available without opening YouTube.

Cookies and analytics

GREXVAN has not enabled a third-party visitor analytics or advertising tracking service in this version, and our website code does not set analytics cookies. Hosting security features and external services you choose to visit may use their own technologies. We will review this policy before enabling any new tracking or online form.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by service providers needed to host and secure the website or handle email. We do not sell information submitted in support or business emails. Third-party services may process information in countries different from your own, subject to their policies and applicable arrangements.

Retention and your choices

We retain inquiry correspondence only as needed for support, business communication, recordkeeping and applicable obligations. This policy does not promise a fixed deletion period. You may contact support to request access, correction or deletion of information you provided. We will review requests in light of applicable obligations and the information we can reasonably identify.

Updates

We may update this policy when website features or practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Wholesale email drafts

The wholesale inquiry form builds a draft in your browser. It does not upload or store the entered fields on this website and uses no tracking cookies. Opening your mail app is not proof of delivery. If you send the draft, your email provider processes it and GREXVAN receives the information to respond to your inquiry.
